# S02E04 — Mines and penal labor system

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E04`

## Evidence

S02E04 establishes that:
- the mines provide metal used by the Silo;
- mining is dangerous and highly undesirable work;
- punishment can include assignment to mining labor.

## Resource/coercion model

```text
critical metal demand
  ↓
dangerous extraction work
  ↓
low voluntary desirability
  ↓
penal assignment supplies labor
```

The project uses the term **penal labor system**, not "penal colony".

Still unresolved:
- mine location and access;
- ore/metal types;
- refining and manufacturing chain;
- sentence duration and release rules;
- proportion of penal vs voluntary workers.
