# S02E02 — Cleaning tape, seal and finite protection

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E02`

## Evidence chain

S01E10/S02E01 already established:
- Juliette receives different tape from the normal cleaning configuration;
- she survives beyond the expected cleaner-death point;
- later she experiences breathing distress while sealed in the suit;
- she can breathe again after opening the helmet inside the second Silo.

S02E02 adds insider testimony:
- the woman involved in replacing the tape believes Juliette eventually died because the suit ran out of breathable air;
- Bernard and Judge Meadows attribute Juliette's survival to the tape replacement;
- Meadows says someone would eventually figure the mechanism out;
- Meadows later conditions her help on receiving the **good tape** before she is allowed outside.

## H34

> Standard cleaning tape is intentionally/systematically inferior, while the alternative "good" tape materially improves seal integrity and exterior survival.

**Confidence:** VH  
**Status:** Strongly Strengthened / Refactored

This is now supported by:
- controlled material difference;
- unexpected survival;
- insider causal attribution;
- insider request for the good tape for personal exterior use.

## Remaining mechanism alternatives

The tape can plausibly affect:
1. external hazardous contaminant ingress;
2. breathing-gas loss;
3. both.

The "air ran out" explanation is still character inference rather than direct engineering documentation.
