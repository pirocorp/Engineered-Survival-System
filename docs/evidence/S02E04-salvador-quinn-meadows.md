# S02E04 — Salvador Quinn, Meadows and the hidden Rebellion-era record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E04`

## Evidence

Before dying, Judge Meadows asks Bernard whether he destroyed a hard drive because it contained material connected to **Salvador Quinn**.

Quinn is described as:
- Head of IT roughly 140 years ago;
- Head of IT during the Rebellion;
- author of a letter that is at least partly encoded.

Meadows also reveals a personal historical discontinuity:
- she was Bernard's shadow;
- about 25 years ago she disappeared for four days;
- she then abandoned the shadow path.
