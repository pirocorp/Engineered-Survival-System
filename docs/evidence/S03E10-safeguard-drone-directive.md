# S03E10 — Safeguard, drones, Pact и Directive

**Knowledge boundary:** `S03E10`

## Silo 17 precedent

Daniel получава briefing, че crisis-ът започва с failed cleaning и cleaner/convict, който преминава expected death zone.

Safeguard е използван като containment response, но mixture delivery се проваля, защото не достига target. Episode dialogue уточнява, че pipe discovery/blockage е historical recurring failure mode и в други Silos.

Това заменя practical reading-а `Safeguard is unbeatable` с по-точен model:
- Silo 1 може да има multiple response options;
- internal pipe delivery е физически interruptible;
- Voice claim за `unbeatable` остава historical institutional claim, не technical fact.

## Drone system

Silo 1 използва aerial drone surveillance.

Drone platform-ът е описан с:
- 30 L poison/Safeguard-mixture payload;
- 2000 rounds ammunition;
- human pilot/operator.

Central control room наблюдава Silo 17 residents при mass exit.

Daniel решава да изчака пълното излизане и след това да използва drones за lethal containment. Sister/operator отказва да стреля, което показва human execution point и възможност за operator non-compliance.

## Containment rationale

Silo 1 doctrine frames cross-Silo spread/contact като `contamination` risk.

Това не доказва biological contamination. Възможните значения включват:
- movement of people;
- information transfer;
- social/political contagion;
- physical/biological threat;
- комбинация.

S03E10 по-късно конкретно свързва Safeguard threat с:
- Second Vault investigation;
- cross-Silo communication.

Следователно informational/social containment е materially strengthened.

## Pact срещу Directive

Direct dialogue:
- residents of Silo 17 choose to leave;
- Pact no longer applies to them;
- Directive remains.

Това поддържа layered governance model:

```text
Pact
→ rules governing life inside ordinary Silo society

Directive
→ higher-order containment/enforcement doctrine
→ survives exit from Pact jurisdiction
→ implemented from Silo 1
```

Не се приема, че `Directive = THE ORDER`, докато няма direct link.

## Silo 18

Silo 18 physically blocks Safeguard pipe.

Silo 1:
- detects blockage/failure;
- establishes drone surveillance over Silo 18;
- orders that anyone attempting to leave be killed.

Това е second observed case на fallback containment pattern и показва, че pipe blockage не освобождава Silo от Silo 1 control.

## Exterior hazard boundary

Silo 1 external poison/kinetic capability е confirmed.

Това силно отслабва assumption-а, че всяка death outside трябва да идва от ambient environment. Но не доказва `outside is safe`.

Cleaning deaths, Silo 17 open-airlock evidence, Bernard no-suit death и Juliette good-tape survival трябва да се обясняват от един по-сложен model.
