# S03E01 — pre-Silo Washington: Daniel Keen, Iran operation и anomalous disruption

**Knowledge boundary:** `S03E01`

S03E01 продължава direct pre-Silo Washington timeline от S02E10.

## Daniel Keen

Congressman-ът от Georgia's 15th congressional district вече е named **Daniel Keen**.

## Retaliatory operation

- Keen има sister, която участва в operation срещу Iran.
- Operation-ът е framed като retaliation за alleged dirty-bomb/radiological attack attributed to Iran.
- Това не resolve-ва S02E10 doubt дали original attack е real или accurately attributed.

## Anomalous disruption

Observed sequence:

Keen's sister survives.

## Interpretation boundary

Common-mode pattern strongly argues against one-aircraft mechanical failure, но не establishes EMP, electronic warfare, radiological/chemical effect, weather, Iranian weapon или any Silo-related technology.
