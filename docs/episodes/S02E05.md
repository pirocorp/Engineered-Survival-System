# S02E05 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E05`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S02E05. Не се използва информация от S02E06+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S02E05 materially clarifies both the **power hierarchy** in Silo 18 and the **continuity infrastructure** in collapsed Silo 17.

Най-големите промени са:

1. Bernard removes Sims as Head of Security.
2. Bernard explicitly tells Sims that Sims will **not** be his shadow.
3. Bernard appoints Sims as the new Judge.
4. This separates the public Judicial office from Bernard's privileged shadow/succession path and shows Bernard can materially reshape Sims' formal role.
5. The Silo 17 survivor says IT has its own independent power supply coming from an external/outside source.
6. Silo 17's main generator failed after a Level 144 pump was blown during the rebellion in an attempt to flood Mechanical.
7. Mechanical failed to restore the generator before rising water reached it; water continues to rise gradually in the present.
8. The survivor wants Juliette to repair a pump that can stop/slow the flooding and power it from IT's independent supply.
9. Level 26 is directly shown again as a repeated spatial anchor.
10. A large multilevel green/common circulation area is directly shown.
11. A newly found Silo schematic contains marked/visible lines associated by scene context with both IT and Judicial.
12. Those lines are compatible with a hidden privileged infrastructure backbone, but the screenshot does not establish whether they carry power, communications, control or another service.
13. A scanned handwritten letter attributed to Salvador Quinn is found in the archive.
14. The letter ends with an encoded/ciphered section, refining the earlier description that it was "partly encoded": the protected payload is specifically at the end.

**Class:** Character technical plan

**Class:** Technical inference from stated plan  
**Confidence:** H.

### Failure/recovery chain

```text
rebellion
  ↓
Level 144 pump destroyed
  ↓
intent: flood Mechanical
  ↓
water keeps rising
  ↓
generator not restored in time
  ↓
generator floods / normal Silo power fails
  ↓
IT remains powered independently
  ↓
possible recovery:
IT power → repaired pump → stop/slow flooding
```

This directly explains why Silo 17 can have powered IT infrastructure after loss of the main generator.
