# S01E08 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S01E08`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S01E08. Не се използва информация от S01E09+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S01E08 materially changes three parts of the working model:

1. **Magnification restriction gets a concrete scientific/medical consequence.** Juliette’s mother built/used a homemade microscope-like device for independent investigation, and a restricted institutional record corroborates authority attention to magnification activity.
2. **Mirror surveillance resolves an earlier explanatory gap.** Juliette realizes authorities could have observed her mother directly, so her earlier belief that her father necessarily informed on her is no longer required.
3. **Institutional procedure can be weaponized at the trigger level.** Mayor and Sims coordinate a trap, claim Juliette said she wanted to go outside, and she is arrested on that basis.

The episode also adds a structured digital messaging datapoint, Level 30, Bernard’s claim that Judge Meadows fears him, and ends with Juliette going over the central-stair railing before the outcome is shown.

S01E08 provides a new mechanism already independently established by the show: **concealed cameras behind mirrors**.

Juliette connects the mechanism to her mother and understands that authorities could have learned about the microscope through surveillance.

Therefore:

```text
OLD CHARACTER MODEL
mother's microscope
      │
      ▼
father informs authorities
      │
      ▼
authorities act

S01E08 ALTERNATIVE / REVISED MODEL
mother's microscope
      │
      ▼
mirror-camera surveillance
      │
      ▼
control / authorities learn directly
```

The new mechanism makes father-as-informant unnecessary for this specific discovery. It does not prove he never provided any other information.

## 8. “She said she wants to go out” — disputed/false trigger

Mayor and Sims claim Juliette said she wanted to go outside.

In the observed interaction:
- Juliette does not make the claimed statement;
- only Juliette, Mayor and Sims are established as present;
- no independent witness is shown.

The claim is therefore tracked as a **false/disputed institutional claim**, not as a statement by Juliette.

This is a critical mechanism because the phrase “I want to go out” has already been established as a trigger with extreme consequences.

Juliette is arrested because of the claim that she said she wanted to go outside.

The chain is:

```text
Mayor + Sims coordinated trap

"I want to go out" procedural trigger
        │
```

This strengthens H25 beyond evidence manipulation after the fact. Here, controlled testimony itself becomes the instrument that creates the institutional predicate for coercion.
