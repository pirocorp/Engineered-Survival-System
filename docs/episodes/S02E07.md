# S02E07 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E07`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S02E07. Не се използва информация от S02E08+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S02E07 materially clarifies the purpose of the secured IT vault, establishes a new direct age anchor for the Silo, shows an explicit anti-IT counter-narrative circulating physically, and independently confirms continuity power for IT in Silo 18.

Най-големите промени са:

1. The secured IT vault includes residential/living compartments, strengthening its interpretation as a true continuity/survival space rather than only a server/security room.
2. Inside the vault is a protected knowledge component called **`Legacy`**.
3. `Legacy` is identified as a library/archive.
4. This gives a concrete mechanism for preservation and intergenerational transfer of privileged historical/technical knowledge to IT leadership.
5. Bernard states that the Silo was built **352 years ago**.
6. Combined with the earlier ~140-years-ago Rebellion anchor, the current chronology places construction roughly **212 years before the Rebellion**, subject to the earlier date being approximate.
7. A handwritten leaflet states that IT lies, that Mechanical wants the truth, asks what happened to Juliette and how Meadows really died, and asks what IT is hiding.
8. This shows a physical anti-IT counter-narrative circulating during the crisis, but the author/distributor is not established by the note alone.
9. During a general Silo 18 blackout, IT remains visibly powered.
10. Residents explicitly notice this: IT's privileged continuity power becomes publicly observable.
11. Silo 18 therefore independently corroborates the broader model that secured IT infrastructure can remain powered when normal Silo power is unavailable.
12. The exact Silo 18 continuity-power source is still unresolved; the episode does not yet prove it uses the same external source described for Silo 17.

## E323–E324 — 352-year Silo age

**E323 —** Bernard states that the Silo was built **352 years ago**.  
**Class:** Character testimony / privileged historical knowledge  
**Confidence:** VH that Bernard states it; H–VH for exact historical precision.

This is the strongest direct age anchor yet for the Silo itself.

**E324 —** Combining the 352-year construction age with the earlier ~140-years-ago Rebellion anchor places construction roughly **212 years before the Rebellion**.  
**Class:** Derived chronology inference  
**Confidence:** H.

```text
Silo construction
~352 years before present
        │
        │ ~152 years
        ▼
Jane Carmody recording
~200 years before present
        │
        │ ~60 years
        ▼
Rebellion / Salvador Quinn
~140 years before present
        │
        │ ~140 years
        ▼
present
```

Derived intervals:
- construction → Rebellion: ~212 years;
- construction → Jane Carmody recording: ~152 years;
- Jane Carmody recording → Rebellion: ~60 years.

The Jane and Rebellion values are approximate testimony anchors, so these are not exact calendar conversions.

This strongly establishes that the Silo existed for a substantial period before the Rebellion.

It still does **not** resolve:
- what `A.R.` literally means;
- how `SILO YEAR 96/97` maps to present chronology;
- whether all Silos were built at the same time;
- whether Bernard's 352-year figure is exact or rounded.

## E328–E329 — Silo 18 IT continuity power

**E328 —** During a general Silo 18 power outage, IT remains visibly lit/powered.  
**Class:** Direct observation + scene context  
**Confidence:** VH.

**E329 —** Residents explicitly notice the exception and ask why IT still has power/light.  
**Class:** Direct dialogue / public observation  
**Confidence:** VH.

Cross-Silo comparison:

```text
Silo 17
main generator lost to flooding
        ↓
IT remains powered

Silo 18
general Silo power outage
        ↓
IT remains powered
```

This independently establishes **functional continuity power** for IT in at least Silos 17 and 18.

What it does not yet establish:
- same exact power source;
- same topology/capacity;
- whether Silo 18 power is external, local generation, storage, or another redundant feed;
- whether every one of the 50 Silos has identical continuity power.
