# S02E01 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E01`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S02E01. Не се използва информация от S02E02+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S02E01 превръща S01E10 multi-Silo reveal-а от exterior observation в директно посещение на втори Silo и показва historical failure mode на поне една друга Silo community.

Основните промени са:

1. Opening historical sequence показва конфликт в **друг Silo**, включително anti-Founder / anti-deception graffiti, IT като defended strongpoint и Sheriff-led група, която достига airlock-а и излиза навън.
2. Juliette намира същия Silo в present day, заобиколен от масово поле от човешки останки, влиза вътре и установява, че Silo-ът не е напълно технически мъртъв.
3. Масовите deaths outside потвърждават **реален lethal exterior hazard**. Exact agent остава unknown.
4. Juliette изпитва acute breathing distress със sealed helmet вътре в другия Silo; след breaking/opening на helmet-а може да диша от interior atmosphere. Това strengthens suit breathing/seal model-а.
5. Вторият Silo има concealed mirror camera architecture, IT zone, agricultural space, airlock, local electrical lighting и secure IT compartment — силен evidence за standardized cross-Silo design.
6. Въпреки mass exterior deaths, поне един **living survivor** е останал вътре в secured IT compartment.
7. Другият Silo е massively flooded до няколко levels под IT.
8. Flashback от детството показва young Juliette при excavation machine в нейния собствен Silo.

## 5. Exterior lethality / suit breathing

Mass exterior remains establish that the outside hazard is **real**, not merely a fabricated fear narrative.

Inside the second Silo, Juliette experiences acute breathing distress while still sealed in the suit/helmet environment. After breaking/opening the helmet, she can breathe the Silo interior atmosphere again.

### Strongest current model

Evidence is most consistent with:

```text
real exterior hazard
        +
suit breathing / sealing matters
        │
        ├─ possible external contaminant ingress through poor seal
        ├─ possible breathing-gas loss / faster depletion
        └─ or both
```

The exact mechanism is unresolved.

An airborne / atmosphere-borne toxin, chemical contaminant, aerosol/particulate or biological agent fits the observed pattern better than pure external radiation as the immediate sole killer. Radioactive airborne particulate is not excluded merely because pure radiation is weakened.

### Important boundary

- exact agent is unknown;
- standard tape sabotage mechanism is unknown;
- suit breathing technology is unknown;
- `oxygen tank`, `rebreather`, `positive pressure` are not accepted facts without direct evidence.

## 6. Cross-Silo surveillance / IT architecture

The second Silo contains a concealed camera behind/in a mirror structure, matching the surveillance concept already confirmed in Juliette's Silo.

This establishes that covert mirror-camera surveillance is **not unique to Juliette's Silo**.

It strongly supports standardized multi-Silo surveillance/control design, while leaving open:
- whether every Silo has identical coverage;
- whether surveillance systems communicate across Silos;
- whether one current central authority controls all Silos.

## 7. IT strongpoint / power / flooding

Present-day exploration shows the second Silo's IT bridge/chokepoint:

The Silo is also massively flooded, with water reaching to within a few levels below IT.
