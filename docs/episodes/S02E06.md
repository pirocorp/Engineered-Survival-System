## Executive update

S02E06 materially clarifies the Silo's internal communications architecture.

Най-големите промени са:

1. A Sheriff Department terminal has an explicit `DIRECT MESSAGING` function.
2. The inbox contains both departmental and named senders, including IT, Office of HR, Mechanical and individual users.
3. The terminal supports an actual two-way person-to-person text conversation.
4. This strengthens the model that the Silo possesses a functioning institutional intranet-like messaging layer rather than relying only on physical couriers.
5. A control-room screen receives a current written field report describing armed-group movement, direction and equipment.
6. The report is routed to named recipients/operators (`ATTN: DOREEN`, `FOR: DIEGO`), supporting a digital operational-reporting workflow.
7. The exact source device/input path used by the informant is not shown.
8. Bernard/IT can cut all radio communications in the Silo, making radio a centrally controllable operational channel.
9. The strongest current communications model therefore contains multiple tiers: physical courier traffic, restricted/institutional digital messaging, and centrally controllable radio.
10. Level **55** becomes a new direct spatial anchor.
11. Level **120** becomes a new direct spatial anchor.

### Communication-layer implication

The safest current synthesis is:

```text
physical courier network
    ↳ broad/general physical message delivery

institutional digital messaging
    ↳ Sheriff / departments / named users
    ↳ access scope still unresolved

radio
    ↳ operational voice communication
    ↳ centrally interruptible by IT
```

The continued use of couriers is therefore no longer best explained by absence of digital technology. Access restrictions, endpoint availability, privacy/audit concerns, physical-item delivery and institutional policy remain competing explanations.
