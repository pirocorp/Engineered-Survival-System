## Executive update

S02E02 materially expands the hidden-control model inside Juliette's original Silo.

Най-големите промени са:

1. Bernard/IT receives a **live exterior video feed associated with Juliette** while she is outside.
2. The feed is lost when Juliette enters the second Silo, establishing a practical transmission boundary.
3. Bernard consults a distinct physical document titled **`THE ORDER`**.
4. `THE ORDER` explicitly states: **`IN THE EVENT OF A FAILED CLEANING, PREPARE FOR WAR`**.
5. Bernard's Silo contains a secured/vault-like IT compartment analogous to the secured IT compartment already observed in the second Silo.
6. Judge Meadows knows about `THE ORDER` and participates in the privileged cleaning/tape knowledge layer.
7. Multiple insiders attribute Juliette's survival to replacement of the normal cleaning tape.
8. Judge Meadows explicitly treats the "good" tape as materially important to surviving outside.
9. Bernard fears that the catastrophic outcome observed around the second Silo could happen in his own Silo.
10. A distinct circled graffiti symbol/emblem appears in rebellion-context imagery; exact meaning remains unknown.

## E217–E219 — tape mechanism becomes insider knowledge

The woman involved in replacing Juliette's cleaning-suit tape believes Juliette died because the suit's breathable air eventually ran out.

Bernard and Judge Meadows attribute Juliette's unexpected survival to replacement of the normal cleaning tape.

Judge Meadows states that eventually someone was bound to figure the mechanism out.

### Model impact

This substantially strengthens the interpretation that standard cleaning tape is not merely accidentally low quality.

The strongest current model is:

```text
standard cleaning tape
        ↓
inferior / inadequate seal
        ↓
limited protection window
        ↓
cleaning can occur
        ↓
protection fails / breathing support degrades
        ↓
cleaner dies under real outside hazard
```

Exact physical pathway remains unresolved:
- external contaminant ingress;
- breathing-gas loss;
- both.

The "air ran out" statement is character inference, not direct technical proof.
