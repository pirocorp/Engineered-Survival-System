# S02E04 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E04`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S02E04. Не се използва информация от S02E05+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S02E04 exposes a second major layer of `THE ORDER`: rebellion management is not only about predicting unrest, but about **directing blame toward Mechanical**.

Най-големите промени са:

1. `THE ORDER` instructs that Mechanical should be blamed during rebellion/crisis.
2. Historical wall markings in Mechanical lead residents to conclude that Mechanical has repeatedly been blamed regardless of where unrest actually began.
3. The most concrete current speculation for *why* Mechanical is selected is its control of the generator/critical infrastructure, but this remains a hypothesis rather than established doctrine.
4. Mining supplies metal used by the Silo; the work is dangerous and undesirable, and penal assignment is used to supply mining labor.
5. The Silo 17 survivor was a child when the rebellion occurred and had already been placed/locked in the IT vault from childhood, refactoring the vault from simple guard post toward continuity/survival refuge.
6. Level **119** is directly observed as a new spatial anchor.
7. Bernard poisons Judge Meadows.
8. Meadows asks about a hard drive containing material related to **Salvador Quinn**, who is described as Head of IT during the Rebellion roughly 140 years ago.
9. Quinn left a letter that is at least partly encoded.
10. Meadows abandoned the path of becoming Bernard's shadow roughly 25 years ago after disappearing for four days.
11. Bernard possesses an immersive headset showing a pre-Silo natural environment identified as Monteverde cloud forest, 2018, and explains that it works similarly to cleaner-helmet imagery.
12. Bernard gives the headset to Meadows before she dies.
13. Bernard stages Mechanical representatives at the death scene and intends to frame Mechanical for Meadows' death, converting the `THE ORDER` scapegoating doctrine into an observed current operation.
14. Bernard claims impeachment protests against Meadows forced him to act and says he knows Sims was behind them.
15. Sims actively turns public sentiment against Mechanical, demonstrating that the hidden-control leadership is not a simple monolithic chain.
16. The episode ends amid large-scale population movement through the Silo during escalating unrest.

### Working question: why Mechanical?

The strongest current candidate is structural:

```text
Mechanical
  ↓
generator / critical infrastructure
  ↓
high leverage over Silo survival
  ↓
dangerous independent power center during unrest
  ↓
preselected crisis scapegoat
```

This remains a hypothesis; other factors such as Down Deep social separation may also matter.
