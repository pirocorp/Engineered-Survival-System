# S02E03 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S02E03`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S02E03. Не се използва информация от S02E04+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S02E03 turns several S02E02 working hypotheses into historically corroborated system behavior.

Най-големите промени са:

1. The survivor in the other Silo states there are **50 Silos** in total and identifies his as **Silo 17**.
2. Juliette's original Silo is strongly identified/inferred as **Silo 18**.
3. Silo 17 provides a concrete historical failed-cleaning case: Ron refused to clean, wrote `LIES`, disappeared from view, and rebellion followed.
4. The survivor says the population later went outside because they did not see Ron die and concluded the outside was safe.
5. This historically corroborates `THE ORDER` warning: **failed cleaning → prepare for war**.
6. The Silo 17 mass-exit deaths are described as caused by a returning airborne/dust-borne poison after the hazard had temporarily dispersed.
7. This separates the ordinary cleaner ~short-window death mechanism from the independently real exterior hazard.
8. The secured IT compartment is explicitly called a **vault**; Russell put the survivor inside and ordered him never to let anyone in.
9. Bernard's physical key `18` opens/accesses the **SERVER ROOM**, and the vault is inside that restricted layer.
10. Bernard says the Jane Carmody cleaning recording is about **200 years old**.
11. Bernard already knew Silo 17 was "dead" long before the current crisis and before Judge Meadows became his shadow.
12. Sims explicitly offers medication so a person can **forget**, strongly corroborating deliberate pharmacological memory suppression.
13. Judge Meadows presents a theory that The Syndrome is a reaction to Silo life rather than a primary physiological disease.
14. Juliette explicitly identifies the lush cleaning imagery as manipulation that induces cleaners to clean; she recognizes the deception because the flying-creature pattern exactly matches the old Jane Carmody recording.
15. A medical system screen exposes **`CODE SILO ORANGE`**, explicitly ordering staff not to remove birth control while ensuring the patient believes it was removed.
16. The same medical record uses **`A.R.`** dating, forcing a chronology refactor: `SILO YEAR 96/97`, `A.R.`, the Rebellion and Bernard's "~200 years" statement can no longer be assumed to share one simple calendar.

## E264 — chronology correction

S02E03 forces a methodological correction.

Previous H15 assumed `SILO YEAR 96/97` and the mayor-journal dating belonged to one post-Rebellion calendar.

That simple model is no longer safe because we now have:
- direct `116 A.R.` institutional dating;
- old HDD material labelled `SILO YEAR 96/97`;
- Bernard's approximate statement that Jane Carmody's recording is ~200 years old;
- the known Rebellion-centered historical chronology.

These cannot yet be reconciled by one simple mapping without additional assumptions.

**E264 — Preserve the chronology conflict explicitly rather than silently remapping the old labels.**  
**Class:** Analytical correction / model-history preservation  
**Confidence:** VH that the prior simple chronology model is insufficient.
