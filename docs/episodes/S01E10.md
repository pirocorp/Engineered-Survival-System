# S01E10 — episode record

**Knowledge boundary:** `S01E10`

Този запис съдържа само observations, dialogue и screenshots до края на S01E10. Не се използва информация от S02E01+, книги, wiki, interviews, leaks или retrospective explanations.

## Executive update

S01E10 resolves the central exterior-visual contradiction and turns several long-running hypotheses into direct or near-direct conclusions.

The major shifts are:

1. **Lush cleaner imagery is false.** Juliette’s helmet presents a lush visual layer that is directly revealed as deceptive.
2. **Barren exterior is substantially real.** When the false layer drops, the devastated landscape remains.
3. **Cleaner mortality is strongly tied to suit sealing.** Juliette receives different tape/material and survives beyond the point Bernard and Sims expect a standard cleaner to die.
4. **Multiple Silos exist.** Wide exterior shots reveal repeated neighboring Silo installations.
5. **Bernard has privileged classified access.** He controls/interrupts sensitive imagery, directs surveillance personnel and can compartmentalize Sims.
6. The illuminated object marked **`18`** is a **physical key**.
7. The exterior includes a distant ruined/city-like skyline.
8. Official Syndrome signage, Level 144 ventilation infrastructure and the Janitorial ROTA add new institutional/worldbuilding evidence.

---

## 1. Classified cleaning footage reaches the monitors

The known cleaning imagery is broadcast across the visible Silo display infrastructure before Bernard stops it.

Bernard then orders control-room personnel not to watch/retain the classified information; Sims is included in the command context.

This establishes **compartmentalization inside the authority structure itself**.

```text
classified cleaning truth
          │
          ▼
Bernard knows / controls access
          │
          ├─ broadcast interruption
          ├─ control-room command
          └─ Sims is not treated as fully read-in
```

This supports Bernard having a privileged layer of access, but does not yet establish that he is the ultimate authority.

---

## 2. Bernard uses the surveillance archive selectively

Bernard personally brings Juliette into the surveillance/control environment and shows her archived incident footage.

Juliette responds that they “never had a chance”; Bernard agrees.

The exchange is evidence of their shared assessment of the power imbalance. It is **not** treated as an objective statement that resistance is universally impossible.

---

## 3. Different tape on Juliette’s suit

Juliette’s cleaning suit uses different tape/material than the standard cleaning configuration.

Bernard and Sims expect her to die around the tree, implying that the normal cleaner failure point is highly predictable.

Juliette survives beyond that point.

### Evidence chain

```text
standard cleaning configuration
        │
        ├─ predictable collapse/death near tree
        │
Juliette configuration
        │
        ├─ different tape/material
        ▼
survives beyond expected failure point
```

This strongly implicates **seal integrity / tape quality** in cleaner mortality.

### Boundary

Still unresolved:
- whether standard tape is deliberately sabotaged or simply systematically inadequate;
- whether external atmosphere is itself toxic;
- exact ingress/lethal mechanism;
- whether any other suit component differs.

---

## 4. Helmet lush view is the deception

Juliette initially sees the same lush representation associated with `JANE CARMODY CLEANING` and concludes that the internal public display is lying.

The episode then directly reveals the opposite: the lush helmet representation is a **false visual layer**.

Bernard recognizes her realization and says, in context, that **she knows**.

### Hypothesis movement

- **H1:** `VH / Confirmed / Refactored`
- **H2:** `VL / Rejected`
- **H3:** `VH / Confirmed`
- **H4:** `VH / Strongly Strengthened`

The precise technology is unknown. “Hologram-like” describes the visual behavior; it is not used as a technical claim.

## 5. Barren exterior and multiple Silos

Once the false lush layer is gone, Juliette sees the barren exterior.

Wide views then reveal repeated circular surface installations consistent with other Silo sites across the surrounding landscape.

A distant ruined/city-like skyline is also visible.

### Model impact

**H10** changes from a weak clue based on `SILO_COUNT` to a direct world-structure conclusion:

> **Multiple Silo installations exist in the surrounding landscape.**

**Confidence:** VH  
**Status:** Confirmed / Refactored

Exact number, status, coordination and governance remain unknown.
