# S03E01 — запис за епизода

**Knowledge boundary:** `S03E01`

Този запис използва само on-screen evidence, dialogue/context и screenshots, предоставени по време на live capture-а. Не се използват web search, books, wiki, interviews, leaks, future episodes, synopses или retrospective explanations.

## Основна промяна

S03E01 премества модела от post-S02E10 crisis към **консолидиран ~3-month post-return state**. Juliette е Mayor, но се оказва едновременно surveilled, pharmacologically memory-suppressed и захранвана с replacement history. Sims family държи dominant local control structure, а context-aware computer/system демонстрира knowledge и operational influence, които изглеждат над ordinary Head-of-IT playbook.

## Bernard — correction chain

Live capture първоначално имаше provisional model, че Bernard е dead след airlock/flame event. Later S03E01 evidence коригира cause:

```text
airlock/flame sequence
        ↓
Bernard survives immediate event long enough
        ↓
Bernard is killed by people
        ↓
six porters / ~six-hour transport
        ↓
Level 67 reconstruction
        ↓
furnaces
        ↓
Sims claims he personally burns Bernard
```

Burning rationale е containment against possible outside-derived lethal contaminant. Това не прави flame system-а `the safeguard`; outside hazard, containment fire и safeguard poison path остават separate mechanisms.

## Computer/system

Direct behavior:
- пита как е Mayor Juliette;
- знае memory-suppression treatment-а;
- казва, че situation е `beyond The Order`;

Това supports supervisory-system model, но **AI identity не е established**. Repo използва `computer/system` или `interactive supervisory system`, докато nature-ът му не бъде direct-confirmed.
